Security & privacy

Built for serious organizations.

Avatars run in private knowledge bubbles. Your data never trains shared models. SSO, SAML, audit logs, and on-prem deployment options are available on Enterprise.

  • SSO / SAML (Okta, Azure AD, Google Workspace)
  • Role-based access control
  • Encryption in transit and at rest
  • Audit-ready conversation history with retention controls
  • Private knowledge bubbles — no shared training
  • On-prem and dedicated-cloud deployment options
