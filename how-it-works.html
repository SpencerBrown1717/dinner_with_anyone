How it works

From question to conversation in three steps.

Choose the avatar you want at the table, interact the way you actually think, and leave with a real conversation grounded in real source material — not another vague chatbot answer.

1

Choose your avatar

A historical figure from the public expert library — Einstein, Caesar, Cleopatra, Confucius, Alexander, da Vinci — or a custom professor avatar built from your own course material.

2

Talk, text, or check in

Type a quick question, hold a real voice conversation, or send a 30-second video check-in. The avatar adapts to your context instead of giving generic advice.

3

Leave with something useful

A clearer idea, a sharper decision, a deeper grasp of the material. Conversations export as memos. For teachers, sessions roll up into a class-wide engagement summary.

What you get

Conversation, not transcripts.

Dinner With Anyone is designed for real questions and real classrooms, not Q&A. Every session is voice-ready, context-aware, and tuned to the way curious people — and engaged students — actually think.
