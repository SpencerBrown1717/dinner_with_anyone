Difficulty controls

Professor help that can meet the student at the right level.

Professors can set the difficulty level so the avatar does not simply give away answers. High school students get more scaffolding. College students get balanced challenge. Graduate and MBA students get sharper questions, stronger pushback, and case-method style practice.

High School

More scaffolding.

Simpler language, more examples, smaller jumps between concepts.

College

Balanced support.

Clear expectations, moderate pushback, real concept-application practice.

Graduate / MBA

Case-method questioning.

Stronger challenge, fewer hints, sharper follow-up patterns.

Professor upload trust

Professors should know exactly what the avatar is allowed to say.

A professor avatar is only useful if the professor can trust it. Dinner With Anyone makes uploaded materials, source boundaries, difficulty level, and answer behavior visible and controllable.

If the source supports it, the avatar answers.

If the source does not support it, the avatar says so.

If the professor restricts it, the avatar respects the boundary.

Student safety

Students learn best when they can safely struggle.

The best practice environment is one where students can mess up, ask basic questions, get frustrated, try again, and improve without embarrassment. Dinner With Anyone helps students build a relationship with a patient mentor, professor, or coach who remembers the learning goal and keeps pushing them forward.
