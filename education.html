How it works

From syllabus to professor avatar in six steps.

No new platform to learn. Upload what you already teach with, generate the avatar, and share a link with the cohort.

1

Upload your curriculum

Slides, syllabi, readings, lecture transcripts, Zoom recordings, PDFs — drop it in. The system handles ingestion and structure.

2

Generate the professor avatar

The avatar is built from your material and your teaching voice — not a generic chatbot. Review it, tune it, publish it.

3

Students interact

By text, by voice, or by sending a short video check-in. The avatar meets each student wherever they're most comfortable.

4

Answers come from class material

Responses are grounded in your actual course content with citations back to slides, readings, and lecture moments.

5

Students submit reflections

Quick check-ins after readings, before discussion, or after exams — text, voice memos, or 30-second video responses.

6

You see what's working

Class-wide engagement summaries: who's keeping up, where confusion is clustering, which concepts need a second pass in lecture.

Use case

A 30-person MBA cohort, on their own time.

Twenty to thirty students can each have a real conversation with the professor avatar before or after class — asking questions they wouldn't raise in a lecture hall, submitting voice memos on the assigned reading, and getting personalized help based on the actual course content.

The professor reclaims office hours, gets a clear view of where the cohort is struggling, and can teach into the gaps the next week.
