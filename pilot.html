The pilot flow

A small, safe first step.

The pilot is designed to be simple enough for one professor, one MBA cohort, or one executive education session.

01

Upload one syllabus

Start with the course map. The syllabus gives the avatar the structure: weeks, topics, assignments, objectives, and vocabulary.

02

Create one professor avatar

The avatar is built around the class material and designed to answer as a course companion, not a generic chatbot.

03

Invite 20–30 students

Students can ask questions before class, review concepts after class, or submit short voice reflections.

04

Students ask by text or voice

The experience is voice-first but flexible: students can type, speak, or eventually submit video check-ins.

05

Teacher gets an insight report

The teacher sees what students understood, where they were confused, recurring questions, and what to review next.

What we need from you

Bring one real class. We'll map the avatar experience around it.

The best pilot starts with material the students are already using. We do not need a perfect dataset. We need enough source material to make the professor avatar useful for one focused learning moment.

Privacy-first pilot

Built for sensitive classroom workflows.

Class material and student feedback can be hosted in a private-server environment for sensitive education workflows. We do not need to publicly expose class data, transcripts, or student submissions.

Private class material Syllabi, slides, transcripts, and readings stay controlled.
Protected student feedback Voice memos, reflections, and check-ins are treated as sensitive.
No compliance overclaiming Privacy-first today, stronger compliance work as the platform matures.
Institution path Hosted demos first, private deployments for larger programs.
