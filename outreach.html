Why this matters

Students do not always ask questions when the professor is in the room.

Dinner With Anyone gives students a lower-friction way to check understanding, prepare for discussion, and reflect after class.

01

Before class

Students ask the professor avatar questions about readings, cases, vocabulary, and frameworks before the live session.

02

After class

Students send voice memos or short text reflections about what they understood and where they are stuck.

03

Before the next class

The teacher sees recurring questions, confusion points, and suggested review topics before walking back into the room.
