From prototype to product

The build path behind this demo.

This page is a static front-end prototype today. Here is the eight-phase roadmap that turns it into a real product.

1

Static demo

Show the avatar, slides, voice states, and classroom upload flow — this page.

2

Real uploads

Teachers upload syllabi, slides, PDFs, transcripts, and lecture notes.

3

Source-grounded professor avatar

Generate a private course knowledge base and a professor avatar that answers from uploaded material.

4

Voice interaction

OpenAI voice for speech input and spoken responses.

5

Slide generation

Gemini turns lectures and readings into slides, summaries, and visual teaching aids.

Avatar rendering

HeyGen or Gemini-style avatar generation creates lifelike professor and historical-figure avatars.

7

Student feedback loop

Voice memos, text reflections, and 30-second video check-ins flow into the teacher dashboard.

8

Private-server deployment

Sensitive institutional workflows hosted on private infrastructure.
