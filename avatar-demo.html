Dinner With Anyone creates educational simulations inspired by public-source or teacher-provided material. Avatars are not the real person and may not represent exact views, words, or private thoughts.

Realism

Why mouth movement matters

A student can forgive a simple avatar. They will not forgive a mouth that feels disconnected from speech. Lip sync, micro-pauses, expression timing, and eye contact are core product quality, not decoration.

Grounding

Why grounding matters

A professor avatar must not confidently invent what the professor said. It should answer from uploaded materials, show source confidence, and say when the answer is outside the approved material.
