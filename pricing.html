FAQ

Questions, answered.

Are the historical avatars actually the real person?
No. They are educational simulations inspired by public-source material — biographies, writings, speeches, lectures, and trusted reference sources. They are interpretive, useful for learning and exploration, and not affiliated with or endorsed by any individual, descendant, or estate.
How are custom professor avatars built?
Teachers upload curriculum, slides, readings, lecture transcripts, and Zoom recordings. The avatar is built from that material, grounded in the actual course content, and can cite back to specific slides, readings, and lecture moments. You review and tune the avatar before students see it.
What's the planned AI stack?
OpenAI for voice interaction (speech-to-text, conversation, text-to-speech). Gemini for slide generation, slide understanding, and curriculum summarization. HeyGen or Gemini-style avatar tooling for the 3D / lifelike avatar layer. Private-server hosting is available for Institution and Enterprise customers.
Can I try it before paying?
Yes — book a demo and we'll walk you through a live session. Explorer also offers a free trial of historical avatars. For Classroom, Institution, and Enterprise plans we usually run a short pilot with a real cohort or team before any commitment.
How is classroom and student data protected?
Class materials, voice memos, and student check-ins are treated as sensitive data. Institution and Enterprise plans unlock private-server deployment so the data lives on infrastructure you control. Dinner With Anyone is designed for privacy-first deployments and is built to support stronger compliance work as the platform matures.
What about enterprise data privacy?
Enterprise avatars can run in private knowledge spaces, on private servers, with admin-controlled access and clear data retention settings. We don't train shared models on your content. Talk to us about the specific deployment shape your organization needs.
Do you offer annual discounts?
Yes — switch to the Annual toggle above to see prices. Explorer, Classroom, and Institution save roughly 20% billed annually. Enterprise has custom annual terms based on usage and number of avatars.
Do you have non-profit or education pricing?
Yes. Email hello@dinnerwithanyone.ai with a short note about your program and we'll get back to you with discounted rates.
Can I cancel anytime?
Yes. Monthly plans cancel at the end of the billing cycle and you keep access until then. No phone tree, no friction.
