FAQ

Questions, answered.

What counts as a session?
A session is up to 30 minutes of focused conversation with one or more experts — voice or text — plus context upload, follow-up Q&A, and an exportable decision memo. Quick follow-ups inside the same thread are free.
Can I try it before paying?
Yes. Free is free forever, no credit card. You can start a real conversation in under a minute and keep the decision memo even if you never upgrade.
Do you offer annual discounts?
Yes — switch to the Annual toggle above to see prices. Pro and Team save 20% billed annually. Enterprise has custom annual terms based on usage and seats.
Can I cancel anytime?
Yes. Monthly plans cancel at the end of the billing cycle and you keep access until then. No phone tree, no friction.
What about data privacy?
Conversations are encrypted in transit and at rest. Team and Enterprise plans include private knowledge bubbles and audit-ready logs. We never train shared models on customer data.
Do you have non-profit or education pricing?
Yes. Email hello@dinnerwithanyone.ai with a short note about your work and we'll get back to you with a discount.
What happens if I run out of sessions mid-month?
You can grab an Extra session pack ($19 for 5 sessions) without changing plans, or upgrade and keep going. Sessions don't expire mid-month — they reset on your billing date.
Can I build a custom expert for my company?
Yes. The Custom expert creation add-on (from $5,000 one-time) builds a digital advisor from your team's IP, voice, frameworks, and decision history. Available on Team and Enterprise plans.
