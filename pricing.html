FAQ

Questions, answered.

Are the historical avatars actually the real person?
No. They are AI simulations inspired by publicly available historical material — biographies, writings, speeches, lectures, and trusted reference sources. They are interpretive, useful for learning and exploration, and not affiliated with or endorsed by any individual, descendant, or estate.
How are custom professor avatars built?
Teachers upload curriculum, slides, readings, lecture transcripts, and Zoom recordings. The avatar is built from that material, grounded in the actual course content, and can cite back to specific slides, readings, and lecture moments. You review and tune the avatar before students see it.
Can I try it before paying?
Yes — book a demo and we'll walk you through a live session. Explorer also offers a free trial of historical avatars. For Student/Class, Institution, and Enterprise plans we usually run a short pilot with a real cohort or team before any commitment.
How do classroom plans handle student privacy?
Institution plans include FERPA-aware privacy controls. Student conversations are surfaced to teachers as aggregate engagement summaries — not raw transcripts — unless a student explicitly shares a session. We never train shared models on classroom content.
What about enterprise data privacy?
Enterprise avatars run in private knowledge bubbles. Your data never trains shared models. We support SSO/SAML, role-based access, audit logs, retention controls, and on-prem or dedicated-cloud deployment for customers that need it.
Do you offer annual discounts?
Yes — switch to the Annual toggle above to see prices. Explorer, Student/Class, and Institution save roughly 20% billed annually. Enterprise has custom annual terms based on usage and number of avatars.
Do you have non-profit or education pricing?
Yes. Email hello@dinnerwithanyone.ai with a short note about your program and we'll get back to you with discounted rates.
Can I cancel anytime?
Yes. Monthly plans cancel at the end of the billing cycle and you keep access until then. No phone tree, no friction.
