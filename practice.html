Difficulty controls

Professors control how hard the avatar pushes.

Difficulty does not mean being harsh. It means matching the challenge level to the learner so practice actually improves performance.

High School

Supportive scaffolding.

Simpler vocabulary, more examples, smaller jumps between concepts, and more encouragement when the student is stuck.

College

Balanced challenge.

Stronger follow-up questions, more independence expected, and concept-application practice that mirrors real course expectations.

Graduate / MBA

Case-method pressure.

Sharper pushback, fewer hints, higher expectations, and questioning patterns students will face in interviews, boards, and executive rooms.

Feedback loop

Practice. Get feedback. Try again.

Every roleplay produces structured feedback so students see what to work on next.

01Choose scenario

Interview, sales, negotiation, office hours, or class discussion.

02Pick difficulty

High School, College, or Graduate / MBA.

03Start roleplay

Voice or text. The avatar pushes back at the right level.

04Receive live pushback

Real follow-up questions, not generic prompts.

05Get score + feedback

Clarity, structure, evidence, source accuracy.

06Repeat until ready

Practice the same conversation until performance is real.
